# Stakeholder Update

Subject: HelpDesk Lite – SLA Auto-Escalation Feature Update

The engineering team has successfully completed the SLA Auto-Escalation feature for HelpDesk Lite.

The feature automatically detects tickets that exceed their SLA deadline and escalates them for immediate attention. This improves response times, reduces the risk of unresolved requests, and supports a more efficient support workflow.

The implementation has passed testing and documentation review and is now ready for stakeholder approval before release.
