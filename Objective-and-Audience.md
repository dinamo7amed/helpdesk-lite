# Objective and Audience Definition

## Objective
The objective is to communicate the new SLA Auto-Escalation feature in HelpDesk Lite clearly so that stakeholders understand its purpose, expected behavior, and business value.

## Target Audience
- Product Manager
- Engineering Team
- Support Team Lead
- Project Stakeholders

## Key Goal
Provide a concise, easy-to-understand update that explains what has changed, why it matters, and what impact it has on the support workflow.
